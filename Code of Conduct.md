# NoMoreInnocenceGROUP — Code of Conduct

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 29.07.2026  

---

# 1. Purpose

NoMoreInnocenceGROUP is a cybersecurity awareness and incident documentation group focused on identifying, documenting, and reporting online threats such as:

- Fraud
- Impersonation
- Social engineering
- Scams
- Account compromise attempts
- Other forms of malicious online activity

The purpose of this group is to improve online safety through responsible documentation, education, and reporting.

Our mission is:

> To protect users through awareness, evidence-based reporting, and responsible cybersecurity practices.

---

# 2. Core Principles

## Evidence Before Accusations

All reports must be based on collected evidence.

Members must:

- Verify information before publishing reports
- Clearly distinguish facts from assumptions
- Avoid unsupported claims
- Preserve original evidence whenever possible

A suspicion is not the same as proof.

---

## Responsible Disclosure

Members should prioritize responsible reporting.

When appropriate:

- Notify affected platforms
- Provide relevant evidence
- Allow time for review
- Cooperate with legitimate investigations

The objective is to reduce harm, not create unnecessary conflict.

---

## User Protection

The safety of potential victims comes first.

Members should focus on:

- Warning users about attack methods
- Explaining how scams work
- Providing prevention advice
- Helping victims understand security risks

---

# 3. Prohibited Behavior

Members of NoMoreInnocenceGROUP must not engage in:

## Harassment

Examples include:

- Threatening individuals
- Encouraging harassment campaigns
- Targeted insults or attacks
- Contacting family members, employers, or unrelated parties

---

## Unauthorized Access

Members must never:

- Hack accounts
- Access private systems
- Attempt password recovery without authorization
- Deploy malware
- Conduct illegal attacks

Investigating an incident does not justify committing another incident.

---

## Doxxing and Privacy Violations

Members must not publish unnecessary private information.

Do not share:

- Home addresses
- Private phone numbers
- Private emails
- Personal documents
- Sensitive personal information

Only information directly relevant to security awareness should be included.

---

## False Reporting

Members must not:

- Fabricate evidence
- Edit evidence to misrepresent events
- Publish knowingly false information
- Frame innocent individuals

Accuracy is more important than speed.

---

# 4. Evidence Handling

All collected evidence should be handled responsibly.

Recommended practices:

- Keep original files unchanged
- Record timestamps
- Document sources
- Maintain a clear timeline
- Separate confirmed facts from analysis

Example:

```
Confirmed:
"The account sent a message containing a suspicious link."

Analysis:
"The link appears to be intended for credential harvesting."
```

---

# 5. Public Reports

Public incident reports should:

Include:

- Incident ID
- Date
- Category
- Description
- Attack method
- Evidence summary
- Prevention recommendations
- Current status

Avoid:

- Personal attacks
- Emotional language
- Unverified accusations
- Unnecessary personal information

Reports should educate, not retaliate.

---

# 6. Interaction With Reported Individuals

If an individual contacts NoMoreInnocenceGROUP regarding a report:

Members should:

- Remain professional
- Avoid arguments
- Request evidence-based responses
- Document communication

Removal requests should only be considered when:

- Information is incorrect
- Evidence is disproven
- Publication creates a legitimate issue

Removal does not automatically mean that previous actions are ignored or erased.

---

# 7. Cooperation With Authorities

NoMoreInnocenceGROUP supports cooperation with:

- Platform security teams
- Cybersecurity organizations
- Relevant authorities

If an incident involves potential criminal activity, evidence may be preserved and provided through appropriate channels.

---

# 8. Member Responsibilities

Members are expected to:

- Act professionally
- Respect privacy
- Follow applicable laws
- Maintain evidence integrity
- Avoid unnecessary escalation
- Prioritize user safety

Being part of this group does not grant permission to perform unauthorized actions.

---

# 9. Enforcement

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- Removal from the group
- Loss of access to internal resources
- Removal of submitted reports
- Reporting of serious violations to appropriate platforms or authorities

---

# 10. Final Statement

NoMoreInnocenceGROUP exists to improve cybersecurity awareness and help users recognize online threats.

We believe that responsible documentation, accurate reporting, and education are stronger tools than retaliation.

Security is built through knowledge, evidence, and responsibility.
